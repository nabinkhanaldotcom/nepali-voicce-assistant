VOICE CONVERSION DEMO

Try the Artist Voice Conversion

You do not need to know the artist or say a specific phrase to try this application. Simply record a short sample in your own voice or upload an audio file and let the trained voice-conversion model transform it.

1

Record or upload audio

Record a few seconds of yourself speaking, or upload an existing audio file (max length - 10seconds).

2

Click Generate Artist's Voice

You can preview your recording first if you want. When you are ready, click Generate Artist's Voice.

3

Wait for the converted voice

Keep this page open while the server processes your recording. When processing finishes, you can listen to the generated voice.

Please note: This project is hosted on a budget-friendly CPU virtual machine to keep things affordable. Voice generation may therefore take a little while, especially when the server is busy. Thanks for your patience while the audio is being processed.
